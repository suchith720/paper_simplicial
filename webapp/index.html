Theoretical Foundations

Why Topological Simplicial Complexes for Scientific Discovery?

Conventional literature retrieval and graph analytics rely entirely on pairwise similarity ($A \sim B$ and $B \sim C$). However, dense continuous embedding spaces suffer from severe semantic drift and intransitivity: two papers connected through an intermediate paper rarely form a coherent cross-disciplinary synthesis on their own.

The Pairwise Limitation

Standard 1D graphs only capture edges ($1$-simplices). They cannot distinguish between open 2-hop chains ($A - B - C$) and closed, mutual three-way interactions where all three pairs independently share strong cosine alignment.

The Simplicial Solution

Lifting the $k\text{NN}$ graph into a 2-simplex (clique or neighbor complex) requires complete mutual adjacency. The filled 2-simplex face represents a genuine higher-order thematic triad capable of yielding revolutionary scientific hypotheses.

The 5-Stage Discovery Pipeline

1
Embedding & Deduplication:

45,666 scientific papers from the CoreX corpus embedded with MatSciBERT. Exact and near-duplicates merged at cosine $\ge 0.99$ yielding 42,164 unique nodes.

2
Undirected kNN Graph (k=5):

PyTorch GPU-accelerated cosine similarity with mean-centering and union symmetrization, producing 166,993 undirected edges.

3
TopoNetX Simplicial Lifting:

Lifting graph edges into 2-simplices using TopoNetX's Clique and Neighbor complex transformations, identifying 94,180 filled triangles.

4
Geometric Tightness Ranking:

Scoring triangles via bottleneck $\text{min}(w) \times (w_1 w_2 w_3)^{1/3}$ combined with English title heuristics to rank the top 1,000 triads.

5
Gemini Pro Reasoning & Hypothesis Formulation:

Feeding 4-section Executive Digests to Gemini Pro to rigorously evaluate conceptual synergy and synthesize 5 testable research statements per triad.
